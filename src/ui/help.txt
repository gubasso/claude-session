claude-session preserves native claude behavior while adding isolated sessions.

Unknown arguments are forwarded unchanged. Use `--` to force every following
argument to the child. Leading `account`, `completion`, `config`, `doctor`,
`help`, `man`, `profile`, and `version` are wrapper-owned. `account login [name]`
delegates saved-login setup to the child; `account list` reports local account
usability. `profile` lists the profiles `--profile` can select, and `config`
reports the resolved configuration and the active profile's composed entry.
`completion` and `man` emit shell completions and a man page for this grammar.
Unknown surfaces remain native passthrough.
