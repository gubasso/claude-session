claude-session preserves native claude behavior while adding isolated sessions.

Unknown arguments are forwarded unchanged. Use `--` to force every following
argument to the child. Leading `account`, `completion`, `doctor`, `help`, `man`,
`profile`, and `version` are wrapper-owned. `account login [name]` delegates
saved-login setup to the child; `account list` reports local account usability.
`profile` lists the profiles `--profile` can select. `completion` and `man` emit
shell completions and a man page for this grammar. Unknown and unimplemented
surfaces remain native passthrough.
